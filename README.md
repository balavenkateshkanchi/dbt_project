About Me 👋
Hi, I'm Kanchi Bala Venkatesh, a Data Engineer with 13+ years of experience in data technologies, specializing in Oracle Database, Snowflake, dbt, SQL, PL/SQL, and Python.

I’m passionate about data modeling, ETL development, and optimizing data pipelines to drive business insights. This open-source dbt project is designed to help beginners get started with incremental models, Slowly Changing Dimensions (SCD), and dbt best practices.

💡 What You’ll Find in This Repo
✅ Implementation of incremental models
✅ SCD Type 1 & Type 2 with practical examples
✅ Best practices for dbt transformations
✅ More enhancements coming soon!

📌 Let’s Connect & Collaborate!
Feel free to explore the repo, contribute, or share your thoughts. Looking forward to learning together! 🚀
